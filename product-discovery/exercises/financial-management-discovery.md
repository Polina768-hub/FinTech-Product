# Financial Management Discovery

## Product Discovery Exercise

### Goal

Apply the Product Discovery framework to investigate the following problem space:

> **Why do young professionals struggle to manage their personal finances effectively?**

The purpose of this exercise is not to create a product immediately.

The purpose is to:

1. Understand the target users.
2. Explore their real behavior.
3. Identify recurring problems.
4. Validate which problems are important.
5. Explore existing alternatives.
6. Identify opportunities worth solving.

---

# 1. Problem Space

## Initial Problem Space

Young professionals often have access to banking applications and financial information but may still struggle to understand and manage their personal finances.

This research aims to explore:

* how they currently manage their finances;
* what triggers them to check their financial situation;
* what difficulties they experience;
* how they currently solve these problems;
* which financial problems are most frequent and important.

---

## Initial Research Question

> **What makes personal financial management difficult for young professionals?**

---

# 2. Initial Assumptions

Before conducting research, I identified several assumptions.

These assumptions are not facts and must be validated.

## Assumption 1

Young professionals struggle to understand where their money goes.

### Importance

High.

### Uncertainty

High.

### How I will test it

Interview users about the last time they reviewed their spending and ask them to describe how they identified where their money went.

---

## Assumption 2

Users often discover that they spent more money than expected only after it has already happened.

### Importance

High.

### Uncertainty

High.

### How I will test it

Ask users to describe the last time they realized they had spent more than expected.

---

## Assumption 3

Existing banking applications provide transaction data but do not always help users understand spending patterns.

### Importance

High.

### Uncertainty

Medium.

### How I will test it

Ask users what information they can currently access and whether they can easily understand changes in their spending.

---

## Assumption 4

Users want to make better financial decisions but do not always know what action to take.

### Importance

Medium.

### Uncertainty

High.

### How I will test it

Ask users what they do after identifying a financial problem and whether they know what action to take.

---

## Assumption 5

Young professionals use multiple tools to manage their finances.

### Importance

Medium.

### Uncertainty

Medium.

### How I will test it

Ask users which tools they currently use and why they use multiple tools.

---

# 3. Target User

## Primary Target User

Young professionals who:

* are approximately 20–35 years old;
* have their own income;
* independently manage personal expenses;
* use at least one banking application;
* make regular financial decisions.

---

## Context

These users may experience financial management challenges when:

* receiving their salary;
* paying recurring expenses;
* checking their bank balance;
* noticing unexpected spending;
* planning financial goals;
* trying to save money.
